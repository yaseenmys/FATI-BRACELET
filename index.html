About Fati Bracelets

Our Story

Welcome to Fati Bracelets — a small handmade jewelry brand based in the heart of Karachi, Pakistan. What started as a passion for crafting beautiful accessories has grown into a loved brand trusted by hundreds of happy customers.

Every bracelet is carefully handcrafted using premium quality beads, threads, and charms. We believe that jewelry is not just an accessory — it's a memory, a feeling, and a piece of art that tells your story.

Whether you're looking for a gift for a loved one, a matching bracelet for your best friend, or something special just for yourself — we've got something perfect for you. 💖

Thank you for supporting our small business!

Frequently Asked Questions

Aap ke sawalon ke jawab

How do I place an order? +

Simply WhatsApp us at 0370 3172066 with the bracelet name and your address. We'll confirm your order within minutes!

Do you deliver all over Pakistan? +

Yes! We deliver all across Pakistan. Delivery time is 2-4 working days depending on your city.

Is Cash on Delivery available? +

Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available all over Pakistan. You can also pay via EasyPaisa or JazzCash for faster processing.

Can I customize a bracelet? +

Absolutely! We love custom orders. Just message us on WhatsApp with your idea, and we'll craft it specially for you.

Do you offer gift wrapping? +

Yes! Every order comes with free beautiful gift wrapping. Just mention it in your WhatsApp message.

What if I don't like the product? +

Customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you face any issue, contact us on WhatsApp within 3 days of delivery and we'll resolve it.
